How a run worksSignals use one-minute market bars with recent history for indicator warm-up. Entries are simulated at bar close, fractional shares are allowed, and commissions/slippage are not included. Each symbol–strategy run has its own starting balance and loss limit; the simultaneous-open cap applies across the queued run. Following the worker’s existing schedule, positions liquidate by 3:55 p.m. ET. Stopping a live run closes paper positions at their latest recorded prices.
